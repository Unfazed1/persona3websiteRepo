About the Series:

Persona 3 Reload is the latest turn-based RPG game released in the Persona series!

Persona 3 Reload is a remake of the 2006 game Persona 3, originally released for the PS2, on modern consoles.

Persona 3 Reload is a turn based game similar to Pokemon in the sense that the protagonist summons mythical Demons and Gods called "Personas" to fight on behalf of them.

Story Synopsis

In terms of story, Persona 3 is about the cycle of life and death. Motifs of death are present throughout the game's dialouge, visuals, and music. You play as a high-school student who, after switching to a school in a big city, falls into a mysterious phenomenon called the "Dark Hour" which is a hidden hour between 11PM and 12AM where people turn into coffins and people's fear of death personify into beings called "shadows." Recently people have been taken into this "Dark Hour" without the potential to summon a persona (which represents the control you have over your fear of death) If one does not have the power of persona, they get consumed by their shadow and become lifeless husks of who they once were, consumed by their fear of death.

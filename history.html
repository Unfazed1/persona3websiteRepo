History of the Series:

Persona as a series has been around for quite some time. In fact, this year it's celebrating it's 40th anniversary since the first Persona game released...all the way back in 1996.

Originally starting as a staple Playstation exclusive RPG series, Persona has grown to become a huge franchise way beyond the original scope of being a niche little spinoff of an even nicher RPG franchise

Spin-off?

That's right, Persona as a series is actually a spin off of the "Shin Megami Tensei" series, which in itself is a spin-off of the "Megami Tensei" series. Atlus just kinda does this sometimes don't worry too much about it as there aren't many references or plotpoints that rely on this connection unless you go REALLY far back in the storyline.

3, the Magic Number

If you've looked at all into Persona as a series you've probably heard (or not heard of) how Persona 3 was a "turning point" for the series. This couldn't be more true, the first 3 persona games (Persona 2 had 2 versions that happened after each other chronologically) and you will find that the gameplay and design was very different from what modern games look like.

The first three Persona games took way more inspiration from SMT (Shin Megami Tensei) than the three modern ones. However, this strategy didn't fully pan out as Atlus had wanted; with bankruptcy looming over the company, Atlus had to make what could possibly be their last game ever their best ever.

Persona 3 ended up being a huge success and a turning point for not only Atlus as a company, but also for the RPG genre as a whole. Persona 3 did so many things differently from other RPG's that it became a standout game despite releasing on old hardware in 2006. Yeah...a PS2 game, after the PS3 had come out.

A Class of It's Own!

Like previously mentioned, Persona 3 was in a class of it's own. From the UI, which used futuristic and modern touches and effects to make the most annoying part of RPG's (usually managing your party) something you look forward too.

The music for the game was also praised as having an original take on not only RPG's but video games as a whole. The battle theme for the game, "Mass Destruction" was unique in so many ways. It used a special Hip-Hop/Rap sound that no other RPG dared to use. The lyricist ,(yes there are lyrics to the songs in this game) Lotus Juice, gave the game a modern sound that was, once again NEVER HEARD OF in the RPG genre...like seriously I can't understand how disruptive this choice was.

Of course, the gameplay was also way different than previous RPG's Atlus had made. The calednar system had been in Persona and even SMT for a long time at this point. However another staple of the Persona series was added with this game...Social Links. Social Links were a way to turn the "daily life" aspect of Persona (which was now more front and center in the gameplay than ever) into a more strategic process. Social Links were essentially levels your friends that would increase each time you spent time with them. These Social Links would benefit you as extra points for when you "fuse personas" (how you create more Personas, we won't get into that tho.)

Sooo Persona 3 was a pretty big game right?

It was just a "big game" it was THE game. The next 2 games in the series (Persona 4 and Persona 5) each got more popular than the rest, but they all take HEAVY inspiration from their roots in Persona 3.

For a long time, the only way to play Persona 3 was with an emulator or the "de-make" of it made for the PSP in 2009 (which got re-released on modern hardware in 2022 dubbed "Persona 3 Portable"/P3P.) However, in 2024 Persona 3 got a full remake made using Unreal Engine, that added modern gameplay elements (like character changing in battle, super attacks dubbed "Therugy Attacks," Fast Travel and more) new music (like the new "advantage battle theme" It's Going Down Now which became ultra popular on it's own) and much more (like voice acting for all "social link episodes)

Looking online, you may find many people complaining or debating on which version of this hit RPG is the best to play, but as a new player who has never played Persona 3 before, Reload is my prefered means to play through this game.
